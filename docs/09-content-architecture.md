# Point 09 — Content Architecture

The content model is conceptually separated by purpose.

## BUILD

Proof of what Faizen has built.

## SOLUTION

An actual Faizen-owned product that people can use.

## SERVICE

A capability to build something new together.

## ARTICLE

Knowledge, SEO, and discovery content.

## HERO

Editorial placement of existing content.

## MEDIA

Reusable visual assets.

## Separation Principle

This separation prevents the website from becoming a generic portfolio/software-house template and keeps content meaning distinct from where that content is displayed.
