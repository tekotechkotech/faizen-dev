# Point 03 — Brand & UX Principles

## Principles

### 1. Do More, Less Explain

This does not simply mean using less text. The interface should communicate substantial meaning through a small number of well-chosen elements.

Requirements:
- One screen should communicate one primary understanding.
- Visuals should carry information.
- Use progressive disclosure where appropriate.
- The primary CTA must be obvious.
- Every component should help the visitor understand Faizen, discover a solution, build trust, or take action.
- Components that do not serve a clear purpose should be removed.

This principle is also a design acceptance criterion.

### 2. Intent Over Persona

Design around what visitors want to accomplish rather than forcing them into predefined persona categories.

### 3. Product First, Company Second

Show what Faizen has actually built and what visitors can use before making the company the subject of the experience.

### 4. Simple Outside, Deep Inside

The public experience should remain simple while the underlying system can support the required content and functionality.

### 5. Partnership Over Transaction

The website should communicate a partnership-oriented relationship rather than treating every interaction as a simple transaction.

## Brand Language

- Brand: **Faizen Studio**
- Descriptor: **Digital Solutions**
- Relationship philosophy: **Let’s build together.**
- Core UX principle: **Do More, Less Explain.**
- Navbar phrase: **BUILD · SOLUTIONS · WITH US**
