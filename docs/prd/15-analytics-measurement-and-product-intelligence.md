# Point 15 — Analytics, Measurement & Product Intelligence

## Purpose

Analytics exists as a feedback loop for understanding visitor behavior and improving the website and product direction. It is not primarily for vanity metrics.

## Questions to Answer

1. Where do visitors come from?
2. What do they view?
3. What are they looking for?
4. What do they click?
5. Do they find a relevant solution?
6. Do they contact Faizen?

## Core Events

- page_view
- view_build
- view_solution
- view_service
- view_article
- click_cta
- get_started
- contact_submit

## Funnel

> **DISCOVER → UNDERSTAND → TRUST → ACT**

Example:

Article → Solution → Get Started → Product/Registration

## Product Intelligence

Measurement should go beyond identifying popular pages.

The system should help answer:

> Why did users take or not take an action?

## Feedback Loop

> User → Website → User Behavior → Analytics → Analysis → Insight → Decision → Improvement → loop.

Avoid unnecessary personal or sensitive data.

## Principle

> Measure behavior, not vanity metrics.

Analytics is part of the initial project requirement. Deeper operational analysis occurs after the website is in production.
